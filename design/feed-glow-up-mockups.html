The feed, three ways.

All four screens show the same four casts, so compare apples to apples. Today's feed — on the left — is a dashed-rail list with run-on bylines, tiny avatars, and dead footer icons. The bones are good; the hierarchy isn't.

Stack: Preact Tailwind 4 daisyUI 5 framer-motion · mini-app sized · Zen mode and Card view already exist as settings.

Shipping in every option

  • Dock — theme-aware glass, not hardcoded rgba
  • NavBar — refresh progress ring + “new casts” pill
  • Feeds picker — hero cards, marks the active feed
  • Settings — grouped, with live preview
  • daisyUI — trim themes: all to a curated set
  • a11y — keyed feed fragments; reply becomes a real button

Page + phones run on data-theme="seymour" — one curated theme, derived from daisyUI dark.
